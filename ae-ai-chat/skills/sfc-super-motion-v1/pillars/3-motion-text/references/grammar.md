# A composable motion grammar

The five axes define mechanism, unit, activation order, local temporal response and final spatial composition. They do not merely multiply colors or industry names. The 100 mechanisms consist of 40 canvas operators, 30 plane/surface or camera treatments and 30 volumetric glyph treatments.

Resolve in this order: preserve the exact source text; shape it; establish valid grouping; measure a final layout; rank activation; assign local delays; evaluate motion; hold a readable state; exit. A different activation sequence never reverses or rewrites the underlying content.

Let i be the original unit index, r_i its activation rank, q_i its measured final position, u its local time progress and v the selected temporal response. Evaluate the family equation using these variables. `s=clamp(v,0,1)` is used when the mechanism cannot accept overshoot. Progress controls represent inputs to an authored rig; they are not a claim that arbitrary vertex deformation has already been computed.

## Compatibility and degeneracy

Whole-block and single-unit input collapse traversal choices. Semantic spans and paired comparisons need editorial boundaries. Spatial traversals need layout first. Variable-font operators require the actual axis; fracture/morph/physics need suitable topology or caches. A conditional record remains indexed but cannot be treated as an implemented clip until requirements are satisfied.

The catalog counts addresses, not guaranteed unique rendered appearances. Track indexed, resolved, engine-generated, rendered and visually reviewed states separately. Original authored defaults are editable design starting points, not measured industry standards.
