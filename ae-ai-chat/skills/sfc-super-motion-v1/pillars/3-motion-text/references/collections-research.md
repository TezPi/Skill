# What the library research contributes

Creator catalogs demonstrate useful vocabulary and organization. Mister Horse separates text treatments by properties and line/word/character units; AEJuice listing labels expose property, unit and easing. Apple Sequence Text is configurable behavior rather than a single fixed preset. Motion Array/VideoHive product listings demonstrate editable controls and output formats. ProductionCrate names generic motion groups; Space Type Generator exposes spatial-module vocabulary.

This skill abstracts underlying mechanisms into original equations and authored composition rules. It includes no downloaded commercial presets, vendor code, fonts or training datasets. Catalog text was read; individual demonstration videos and native projects were not executed. Names, counts and software claims on a listing are not evidence of visual validation or industry-wide usage frequency.

The Kinetic Typography Engine supports a compositional approach to expressive temporal typography. Kinetic Typography Diffusion Model distinguishes generated samples from authored templates; the distinction informs honest count reporting. Dynamic Typography discusses semantic deformation and readability in its specific research method, not a guarantee that arbitrary AI video tools produce correct text.

Consult data/sources.json for URLs, provenance and exact scope. Sources support primitives and reasoning, not the claim that a vendor supplied the specific million synthesized recipes in this package.
