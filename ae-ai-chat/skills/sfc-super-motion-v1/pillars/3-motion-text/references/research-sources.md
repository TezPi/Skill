# Research sources and evidence scope

Research access date: 2026-10-28. See the machine-readable source registry in `../data/sources.json` for all entries and verification levels.

Primary technical sources include [Adobe text animation](https://helpx.adobe.com/after-effects/desktop/animating-text/text-animation/animating-text.html), [GSAP SplitText](https://gsap.com/docs/v3/Plugins/SplitText/), [Remotion frame animation](https://www.remotion.dev/docs/animating-properties), [Three.js TextGeometry](https://threejs.org/docs/pages/TextGeometry.html), [Maxon Text Object](https://help.maxon.net/c4d/2026/en-us/Content/html/OMOGRAPH_TEXT.html), [Unicode UAX29](https://www.unicode.org/reports/tr29/) and [ECMA-402 Segmenter](https://tc39.es/ecma402/#segmenter-objects).

Collection research includes [Mister Horse text presets](https://misterhorse.com/category/text-presets), [AEJuice previews](https://aejuice.com/product/text-animation-presets/previews/), [Apple Sequence Text](https://support.apple.com/guide/motion/apply-the-sequence-text-behavior-motn1607c46e/6.4/mac/26.6), [ProductionCrate](https://www.productioncrate.com/archives/ae-scripts/productioncrate-text-animator), [Motion Array](https://motionarray.com/motion-graphics-templates/in-control-kinetic-typography-1800284/), [Premiumilk on VideoHive](https://videohive.net/item/kinetic-neon-typo-pack/39609087) and [Space Type Generator](https://spacetypegenerator.com/).

Research foundations include [Kinetic Typography Engine](https://www.cs.cmu.edu/~johnny/academic/KT_Engine_UIST2002.pdf), [Kinetic Typography Diffusion Model](https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.10476) and [Dynamic Typography](https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.11614). Data interpretation and accessibility references are linked in the source registry.

Most technical pages and creator catalog text were read. Paper abstracts are marked separately where applicable. Blender D11-D13 were verified only through official indexed content because full page retrieval failed; their versions vary and exact current nodes/APIs remain unverified. Motion Design School was inaccessible and is not used as evidence for a specific course. No individual demo playback, native project execution, rendered-font validation, user study or popularity measurement was performed.

All operator names, equations, parameter defaults, industry profiles and the million-factor grammar are authored design inferences. They are not copied vendor presets. The catalog is unrendered; structural validation is reported separately from visual evaluation.
