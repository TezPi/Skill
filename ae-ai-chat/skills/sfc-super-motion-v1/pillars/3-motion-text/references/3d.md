# Flat spatial text and real 3D typography

Families F040-F069 use planes/surfaces or cameras and are classified 2.5D in this skill. F070-F099 require volumetric glyph/relief geometry. A perspective transform, CSS shadow or billboard is not sufficient to claim solid glyph sides.

Declare representation, positive depth, bevel, subdivisions, normals, counters, pivot hierarchy and grouping after shaping. Declare camera projection/position/target/FOV/near/far/framing, material response and key/fill/rim lights. Surface/ribbon deformation additionally needs UV continuity; fracture, shell and morph need correspondence; simulations need actual colliders and a baked cache.

Use one shared camera/light rig for shot-level motion. Traversal controls visibility or an independent local accent, not one camera per letter. Orthographic card stacks use orthographic projection and cannot rely on perspective size changes. The counter-portal family needs an actual open counter, clearance, a safe camera path and a supplied destination scene; it runs after the readable title hold.

Before claiming a final render, inspect first-visible, maximum displacement, midpoint, start/end of reading and exit. Verify side geometry from another angle, correct normals, counter openings, no mirrored backface, no z-fighting/near clipping, and readable highlights/depth of field. JSON/schema validation cannot establish these properties.
