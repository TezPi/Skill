# Mix and extend beyond one million

The catalog is a starting grammar. The AI is authorized to adapt and invent within the requested brief without permission for each variant. A new structure should change a meaningful relation, trajectory, deformation, topology or staged action. Merely changing a font/color/seed is a customization, not proof of a new motion concept.

## Construct a mix deliberately

1. State the semantic purpose and exact content invariants.
2. Select parent IDs and choose a primary mechanism. Resolve their coordinate spaces and final rest poses.
3. Assign ownership per channel: a wrapper may translate while a child reveals; a shared camera belongs to the shot. For conflicting transforms choose an explicit override, additive offset or multiplicative scale, with the correct neutral value.
4. Choose sequential, overlapping or conditional stages and define the time domains. Keep entrance, meaningful emphasis, reading and exit distinct. Do not blindly add two oscillations or two camera rigs.
5. Describe concrete motion with equations/control points, units, pivots, masks, materials, geometry and requirements. Preserve a legible final state and a reduced/static alternative.
6. Record parent IDs, what changed, why it fits the content, seed, constraints and actual validation state. Use a CUST identifier rather than impersonating an indexed MT ID.

Example: a padded matte reveals a phrase, the fully formed phrase grows real extrusion, one light sweep accents its relief, and a stable read interval follows. Separate opacity/reveal, geometry depth and light position controls. If the selected tool cannot create volume, propose an explicit planar alternative with a different ID and reason.

The CLI custom command supports deterministic sequential stages from existing families with durations, parameters and blend labels. It records a descriptive choreography; it does not solve channel conflicts or render it. For parallel graphs, new operators or advanced relations, author an additional specification using `examples/custom-template.json`; include equations, stage graph and implementation requirements. Such specifications may exceed the base 100 families and are not limited by the million-ID grid.
