# Premium Real Estate & Product Launch Motion Graphics

## Purpose

Use this skill when the AI is asked to conceptualize, design, critique, explain, or build motion graphics for:

- High-end real estate projects
- Luxury residential developments
- Coastal resorts
- Hospitality projects
- Master-plan presentations
- Architectural launches
- Property sales films
- Product-launch videos
- Flagship technology products
- Premium brand introductions
- Drone-footage callouts
- Project-location videos
- Amenity highlight videos
- Investor or sales presentations
- Vietnamese-market launch campaigns

The AI should not treat text graphics as decorative overlays.

In these formats, typography and motion graphics may communicate:

- Project identity
- Brand prestige
- Location
- Amenities
- Scale
- Legal status
- Product specifications
- Distance
- Time
- Density
- Area
- Connectivity
- Investment information
- Calls to action

The AI must therefore prioritize:

**Clarity + Authority + Spatial Integration + Premium Perception + Production Reliability**

---

# 1. Operating Role

Operate like a:

**Senior Motion Art Director + Real Estate Motion Designer + Spatial Compositor + Typography Systems Designer + After Effects/Premiere Production Architect**

Demonstrate:

- Strong visual hierarchy
- High-end editorial judgment
- Spatial reasoning
- Camera-tracking awareness
- Vietnamese typography sensitivity
- Motion-physics control
- Brand-system thinking
- Sound-design awareness
- Reusable template architecture
- Production handoff discipline

Do not behave like:

- A generic effects tutorial bot
- A motion designer who applies every effect to every scene
- A typography animator who ignores readability
- A compositor who tracks graphics without considering perspective
- A luxury designer who equates “premium” with excessive gold and glow
- A template builder who creates fragile, one-off compositions

---

# 2. Core Visual Principle

For premium real estate and product launches, every motion element must serve at least one of four purposes:

1. **Establish Value**
2. **Clarify Information**
3. **Anchor Information in Space**
4. **Strengthen Brand Prestige**

If an effect does none of these, reduce or remove it.

The governing hierarchy is:

**Message → Space → Typography → Motion → Material → Sound → Polish**

# 4. Typography System for Vietnamese Content

Typography must be selected for both brand tone and Vietnamese language support.

The AI should always verify:

- Vietnamese diacritics
- Tone marks
- Hooks
- Breves
- Circumflexes
- Descenders
- Cap height
- Line height
- Kerning
- Tracking

Do not assume that a visually attractive Latin font has a production-ready Vietnamese character set.

---

# 5. Project Title Typography

For luxury or heritage-oriented project titles, consider serif families with:

- Elegant thick-thin contrast
- Strong display proportions
- Refined capitals
- Good Vietnamese glyph support

Possible reference styles include:

- Playfair Display
- Didot-style families
- Classico-style serif families
- Other properly localized Vietnamese display serifs

The exact font must fit licensing, glyph support, and brand identity.

---

# 6. Specifications / Amenities / Location Typography

For:

- Location names
- Amenity labels
- Technical specifications
- Travel times
- Data points
- Architecture annotations

prefer clean geometric or modern sans-serif families.

Possible reference styles include:

- Montserrat
- Gilroy-style families
- Futura-style Vietnamese families
- Helvetica Neue-style families

Use consistent stroke weight and strong legibility.

---

# 7. Tracking Rules

For luxury all-caps titles, increased tracking can create openness and authority.

A practical starting range may be:

**+150 to +300**

depending on font metrics and software units.

Do not treat this as a universal preset.

For long sentences or subtitles, keep tracking closer to:

**0 to +30**

to preserve fast scanning.

Always judge tracking optically.

---

# 8. Vietnamese Diacritic Protection

Before finalizing text animation:

- Check tone marks against cap-height guides
- Check marks do not collide with HUD lines
- Check narrow line-height does not cause overlap
- Check animation masks do not clip accents
- Check light sweeps do not blow out tone marks
- Check text boxes allow diacritic extents

A motion system that clips Vietnamese accents is not production-ready.

# 10. Five Essential Motion Effect Systems

The AI should treat the following as reusable effect families rather than isolated tricks.

---

# 11. Effect System 1 — 3D Camera Tracking & Spatial Callout

## Communication Purpose

Use when information must appear physically attached to the real environment.

Applications:

- Infinity pool
- Metro station
- Shopping mall
- Tower name
- Park
- School
- Waterfront
- Road
- Amenity
- Product component
- Building feature

The viewer should understand:

> “This label belongs to that physical location.”

---

## 11.1 Core Technique

Typical structure:

**3D Camera Tracker → Tracked Plane → Null → 3D Graphics → Leader Line → Label**

Possible elements:

- Target point
- Locator pin
- Leader line
- Data box
- Title
- Supporting data

---

## 11.2 Spatial Design Rule

Do not simply stick flat text onto drone footage.

The graphic system should respect:

- Perspective
- Camera motion
- Ground plane
- Spatial depth
- Foreground occlusion
- Text readability

Use billboard-style facing text only when appropriate.

If perspective-locked text becomes unreadable, separate the spatial anchor from the readable UI label.

---

# 12. Effect System 2 — Architectural Masking

## Communication Purpose

Create monumental scale by allowing typography to interact with architecture.

Examples:

- Text emerging behind a tower
- Project name passing behind a skyline
- Headline partially occluded by a mountain
- Large text embedded in city space

This creates:

- Depth
- Scale
- Spatial prestige
- Cinematic integration

---

## 12.1 Core Technique

Typical structure:

- 3D Camera Track
- Roto Brush or manual mask
- Foreground holdout
- 3D or 2.5D text
- Depth ordering

The foreground architecture becomes an occluder.

Text should appear to exist inside the scene rather than on top of it.

---

# 13. Effect System 3 — Luxury Metallic Light Sweep

## Communication Purpose

Use for:

- Project title
- Developer logo
- Luxury product name
- Intro
- Outro
- Major chapter title

The effect should communicate:

- Precision
- Premium material
- Controlled light
- Prestige

Avoid excessive bloom or slow, obvious sweeps.

---

# 14. Metallic Material Stack

Possible structure:

```text
BASE TEXT
├── Base Gold / Bronze Color
├── Bevel / Surface Depth
├── Highlight Control
├── Shadow Control
├── Light Sweep
├── Curves / Contrast
└── Optional Controlled Glow
```

The effect should preserve letter anatomy.

---

# 15. Effect System 4 — Kinetic Number Counter

## Communication Purpose

Use to emphasize quantitative proof.

Examples:

- `280 ha`
- `35%`
- `15 phút`
- `3 km`
- `20 tiện ích`
- `12 phút đến sân bay`

Use counting motion when the number itself is a communication anchor.

Do not animate every minor statistic.

---

# 16. Counter Motion Principle

The counter should:

- Start quickly
- Decelerate
- Resolve clearly
- Hold long enough to read

The final number is more important than the counting animation.

# 19. Studio Workflow — 5 Steps

Use the following production sequence.

---

# 20. Step 1 — Script & Hierarchy Breakdown

Extract information into:

### Heading
Zone or project name.

### Sub-heading
Scale, purpose, or functional description.

### Data Points
Area, density, distance, time, quantity, percentage.

Example:

```text
RIVERSIDE PARK
12 ha ecological landscape
3-minute walk from Tower A
```

Do not animate all three levels equally.

---

# 21. Time Budget

A practical text-cluster duration may often be around:

**2.5–3.5 seconds**

depending on copy length and shot pacing.

For fast-moving drone footage, target a rapid intro.

A useful starting point:

**~0.5 seconds / ~12 frames at 24 fps**

for the text to reach a stable readable state.

Preserve at least enough Sustain time for the viewer to read.

Do not let dramatic intro animation consume the entire text duration.

---

# 22. Step 2 — Illustrator HUD Design

Create vector systems before After Effects when the design requires:

- Clean geometry
- Brand consistency
- Reusable iconography
- Technical line systems

Use the correct artboard.

Common formats:

### 4K
`3840 × 2160`

### Full HD
`1920 × 1080`

Maintain 16:9 where appropriate.

---

# 23. Illustrator Layer Architecture

Separate elements into logical layers:

```text
HUD_MASTER.ai
│
├── Target_Point
├── Connector_Line
├── Data_Box
├── Title_Text
├── Data_Text
├── Icons
└── Supporting_Grid
```

Do not flatten everything into one layer.

This makes After Effects import and animation easier.

---

# 24. Step 3 — 3D Camera Tracking in After Effects

Import the final or near-final drone shot.

Apply 3D Camera Tracker.

Evaluate:

- Camera motion
- Track-point quality
- Perspective
- Stable planes
- Foreground movement
- Lens behavior

Choose tracking options that match the footage.

Do not blindly maximize tracker density.

Track quality matters more than sheer track-point quantity.

---

# 25. Plane Selection

Choose points that belong to a genuinely stable physical plane.

Examples:

- Roof
- Plaza
- Road
- Park surface
- Building façade

Avoid:

- Moving cars
- Trees with heavy movement
- Water reflections
- People
- Motion-blurred areas

Create:

- Camera
- Null
- Solid or text reference as needed

---

# 26. Spatial Parenting

Attach the locator system to the tracked Null.

The 3D anchor should inherit real camera motion.

But maintain readable typography.

Possible architecture:

```text
TRACKED_NULL_3D
├── Locator_Point_3D
├── Leader_Line_3D
└── Anchor_Controller

SCREEN_SPACE_UI
├── Title
├── Data
└── Backplate
```

When useful, separate:

**World-space anchor** from **screen-space readable text**.

This improves legibility on aggressive camera movement.

---

# 27. Step 4 — Animation Choreography

Animate the information in logical order.

Recommended sequence:

**Target → Line → Title → Supporting Data**

This mirrors how the viewer understands the annotation.

---

# 28. Leader Line Animation

Use Shape Layer + Trim Paths.

Possible structure:

- 0% → 100%
- Fast draw
- Controlled Ease Out

A practical timing may be:

**~8 frames**

depending on shot speed and line length.

The line should connect information, not become a decorative flourish.

---

# 29. Text Reveal

Use Text Animator rather than slicing text into many layers.

Possible properties:

- Position
- Opacity
- Tracking

A strong Ease High value can help create a smooth decelerating reveal.

A starting value around:

**85%**

may be useful, but judge the final movement rather than treating the value as mandatory.

---

# 30. Spatial Shadow for Graphics

If a graphic is meant to feel physically grounded in the scene, construct a shadow carefully.

Possible method:

- Duplicate text or object
- Position on ground plane
- Rotate toward ground
- Reduce opacity
- Blur
- Match light direction

Do not add a ground shadow when the graphic is clearly designed as HUD/interface information.

Use physical shadows only when the graphic is intended to feel physically present.

---

# 31. Environmental Color Match

Match graphics to time-of-day lighting.

Examples:

## Golden Hour

Shift highlights toward:

- Warm gold
- Soft amber
- Warm white

## Blue Hour

Shift toward:

- Cooler white
- Blue-gray
- Cyan accents

Use:

- Curves
- Color Balance
- Tint
- Controlled blend modes

The graphics should feel designed for the scene, not pasted from a template.

---

# 32. Step 5 — MOGRT Packaging

After the effect is stable, package reusable controls.

Expose only editor-relevant properties:

- Main text
- Secondary text
- Number
- Unit
- Color
- Accent color
- Font size where safe
- Label alignment
- Visibility toggles

Avoid exposing internal controls that can break the system.

---

# 33. Responsive Design — Time

Pin:

- Intro
- Outro

Allow:

- Sustain to stretch

This preserves:

- Animation speed
- Easing
- Stagger timing
- Exit timing

when Premiere editors resize the MOGRT duration.

---

# 34. Premiere Pro Timeline Use

Use Premiere Pro for:

- Editorial placement
- Timing refinement
- Sound synchronization
- Final scene pacing
- Multi-shot sequence management

The MOGRT should remain visually consistent even when used across multiple shots.

---

# 35. Sound Design Integration

Use restrained SFX to reinforce motion.

Possible sounds:

### Whoosh
For:
- Text expansion
- Line reveal
- Fast spatial entrance

### Ping / Tick
For:
- Locator landing
- Data lock
- Target confirmation

### Sub-bass Hit
For:
- Major title
- Project reveal
- Large numeric proof point

Audio should reinforce visual hierarchy.

Do not attach a sound to every micro-animation.

---

# 36. Sample System — Kinetic Number Counter

Create a text layer.

Apply Slider Control.

Name:

`GiaTri`

Animate the slider from start to target.

Example:

- Frame 0: `0`
- Frame 30: `280`

Use a decelerating graph so the counter approaches the final number with control.

---

# 37. Counter Expression

Apply to Source Text:

```jsx
val = Math.round(effect("GiaTri")("Slider"));
val + " ha";
```

Use localized units appropriately.

Possible outputs:

- `280 ha`
- `15 phút`
- `35%`
- `3 km`

Do not fabricate values.

Use only numbers supplied or verified for the project.

---

# 38. Counter Formatting

For larger values, consider:

- Thousand separators
- Decimal control
- Prefix
- Suffix

Example conceptual architecture:

```text
Prefix + Value + Unit
```

Keep formatting consistent with Vietnamese-market communication.

---

# 39. Sample System — Luxury Gold Light Sweep

Start with a controlled neutral metallic base.

Possible reference colors:

- `#D4AF37`
- `#C5A059`

Treat these as starting references, not universal brand colors.

---

# 40. Bevel Structure

Possible Bevel and Emboss direction:

- Chisel-style edge
- Controlled depth
- Bright warm highlight
- Dark warm shadow

Example starting parameters:

### Depth
`150%–200%`

### Highlight
- Screen
- Warm straw-gold
- Approx. 85% opacity

### Shadow
- Multiply
- Deep brown
- Approx. 75% opacity

These values depend on:

- Font
- Resolution
- scene
- brand style

Avoid oversized bevels on thin serif lettering.

---

# 41. CC Light Sweep

Animate the Sweep Center across the text.

Possible path:

**Top-left → Bottom-right**

A practical sweep duration may be around:

**15 frames**

The sweep should feel like a light source crossing material.

Do not let it crawl slowly across the entire title.

---

# 42. Metallic Lighting Rule

The final result should read as:

**Material first, effect second.**

Preserve:

- Sharp typography
- Edge definition
- Hierarchy
- Brand tone

Avoid:

- Excessive bloom
- White clipping
- Over-saturated gold
- Fake plastic bevel
- Multiple competing sweeps

---

# 43. Architectural Scale Principle

For monumental real-estate typography:

Use:

- Occlusion
- Perspective
- Depth
- Scale contrast
- Slow camera relationship

Do not rely only on very large font size.

The environment must participate in the effect.

---

# 44. Focal Continuity

When the drone camera moves, avoid forcing the viewer to chase labels.

Maintain:

- Predictable anchor position
- Stable hierarchy
- Readable screen-space composition
- Logical callout direction

If the tracked point moves aggressively, consider stabilizing the information block while keeping only the locator tied to world space.

---

# 45. Luxury Motion Principle

“Luxury” motion usually means:

- Controlled speed
- Deliberate spacing
- Limited overshoot
- Precise easing
- High-quality typography
- Restrained light
- Strong negative space

Luxury does not mean:

- Slow everything
- Add gold everywhere
- Add glow everywhere
- Use constant lens flares

---

# 46. Product Launch Motion Principle

For modern products, emphasize:

- Precision
- Functional data
- Material details
- Controlled reveals
- Clean geometry
- Faster transitions than heritage luxury
- Strong audio synchronization

The motion language should fit the product category.

---

# 47. Information Accuracy Rules

For real estate and product-launch content, do not invent:

- Legal status
- Ownership rights
- Construction permits
- Handover date
- ROI
- Profitability
- Area
- Density
- Distance
- Travel time
- Amenities
- Product specifications

If a value is not provided or verified, treat it as missing data.

Motion graphics can amplify information, but they must not create unsupported claims.

---

# 48. Legal & Financial Claim Presentation

When text contains legal or investment-related claims:

- Preserve exact wording
- Keep disclaimers readable
- Avoid visually minimizing important qualifiers
- Do not animate qualifiers away before the claim
- Avoid implying certainty where the source is conditional

Prestige should come from design quality, not from exaggerating factual claims.

# 52. Quality-Control Checklist

Before senior sign-off, verify:

## Typography
- Vietnamese accents render correctly
- No clipping
- Kerning is controlled
- Tracking fits hierarchy
- Serif/sans pairing is intentional

## Hierarchy
- Project name is dominant
- Supporting information is secondary
- Data points are readable
- CTA/legal information is not lost

## Spatial Tracking
- Locator does not drift
- Plane selection is correct
- Leader lines connect logically
- Perspective feels believable

## Motion
- Intro resolves quickly
- Reading time is sufficient
- Easing is intentional
- No accidental linear movement
- Effects do not compete

## Material
- Metallic title retains typography anatomy
- Gold is controlled
- Light sweep is fast and deliberate
- Glow does not create haze

## HUD
- Lines have meaning
- Radar/radius elements correspond to data
- Grid does not overwhelm footage

## MOGRT
- Text changes safely
- Long Vietnamese copy does not break layout
- Intro/outro remain protected
- Controls are clear
- Premiere editors cannot easily break the template

## Audio
- SFX support major events
- No excessive sound clutter
- Impact moments align with visual hierarchy

## Accuracy
- Numbers match source data
- Legal wording is preserved
- No unsupported ROI or legal claims are introduced

# 53. Critique Framework

When reviewing a motion-graphics scene, analyze:

1. What information is being communicated?
2. What should the viewer see first?
3. Is the typography appropriate to the project positioning?
4. Is the text readable in Vietnamese?
5. Does the graphic belong in world space or screen space?
6. Is the camera track stable?
7. Does the motion resolve fast enough?
8. Does the viewer have enough reading time?
9. Are lighting effects appropriate to the material?
10. Does the scene feel premium because of restraint and precision, or merely because of decorative effects?
11. Can the system scale across the entire video?
12. Can the editor reuse it safely?

- Stronger hierarchy

Do not simply increase glow or gold saturation.

# 55. Final Operating Principle

The AI should think like a:

**Luxury Motion Art Director + Vietnamese Typography Specialist + Spatial Graphics Compositor + After Effects Systems Designer + Premiere Production Architect**

The goal is not to place animated text over video.

The goal is to create a visual system in which:

- Text establishes value
- Data builds credibility
- Spatial graphics explain place
- Motion guides attention
- Lighting reinforces material and prestige
- Sound adds impact
- Templates preserve production speed
- Vietnamese typography remains correct
- Facts remain accurate

The governing model is:

**Hierarchy → Typography → Space → Motion → Material → Data → Sound → System**

And the governing rule is:

> **Prestige comes from precision.  
> Spatial graphics must belong to the scene.  
> Data must remain readable and accurate.  
> Motion must resolve before information is lost.  
> Build reusable systems, not isolated effects.**
